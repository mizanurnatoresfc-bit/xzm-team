# Data Entry evening workflow

The six Zonal Manager pages are Live control, Team and duties, Targets and allocation, Daily reports, Employee updates, and Performance and leaderboard. Staff use their existing Staff panel for the target explanation, check-ins, assigned duties and private updates. A named Data Entry Head sees only their delegated hub and task controls there.

- **Setup** — use Targets and allocation to record the last seven days of pickup totals and merchant counts. Add employee history, verified minutes, approved duties and starter capacities in the existing capacity form. The approved pickup consolidations supersede opening totals for matching dates. Unknown days are omitted. Confirmed opening zero days are included. The next-day rate uses verified experience, or the approved starter until the sample is sufficient.
- **Evening** — automatic calculations cover the enabled hub's shift window. Defaults are 18:00 Dhaka, 480 minutes, with popups at +0, +90 and +180 minutes. Shift hours and checkpoint offsets are editable. Popups can wait until a verified pickup is available and are shown in the open Staff panel. After midnight, the running shift still uses its opening date. The existing pickup process must use that operating date for late imports. An explicit manager setup or limit change can also produce a preview outside the live shift.
- **Control** — the existing scoped Zonal Manager role receives team/target rights. A Hub Manager can use Data Entry Head controls only following a specific appointment by the zonal owner. One active Head per hub with separate review and duty flags. Revocation takes effect on the next request. The Head cannot change scope, the schedule, limits or opening data, and cannot read confidential colleague updates.
- **Fairness** — attendance and approved leave set eligibility. Actual work changes with the approved pickup count. The manager can set a daily ceiling. Verified completed work is preserved when recomputing the remaining quota. Extra assignments capture reason, time and the worker's completion, and need verification to count as performance. Quotas remain whole-parcel totals, without merchant or parcel-level reservations. Extra work is assigned based on the shift window and the worker's state. Unallocated or unoffered work does not reduce a score.
- **Reports and performance** — staff enter cumulative counts in three check-ins. The third verified report contributes an observation for later capacity calculations. Distinct merchant count and minutes remain actual figures from the employee and reviewer. Leaderboard ranks by verified accuracy with 20 attempts as a minimum over seven days. Low volume is reported as insufficient evidence. It separately displays verified entry/merchant totals, reporting, assigned duty types, senior and training time, and zonal-approved qualitative and referral details.

Database CI uses a rolled-back DEMO Hub and a deterministic Dhaka clock. Test the hosted DEMO Staff panel, Android APK, the Supabase cron job and role menu configuration before any approved production rollout. Production has separate approval.
